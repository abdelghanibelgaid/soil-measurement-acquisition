# Data provenance

Primary executable data: `ortiz_tomato_covs.csv`.

Source documentation: agridat `ortiz.tomato.covs`, based on Ortiz, Crossa, Vargas, and Izquierdo (2007), Euphytica 153:119–134.
Raw public mirror used to reconstruct the exact file: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vincentarelbundock/Rdatasets/refs/heads/master/csv/agridat/ortiz.tomato.covs.csv

The fertilizer variables ExN, ExP, and ExK are recorded extra nutrient amounts at each environment. They are used as observable historical reference actions, not causal agronomic optima.
